# Kannur Insta Growth

Helps a local repair shop turn Instagram into a steady source of followers and walk-in customers. The audience is people in and around Kannur (students, office workers, small businesses, gamers, parents) who will one day have a slow laptop, a dead hard disk or a broken screen. The goal is not a big vanity number: it is followers who live nearby and remember the shop when something breaks.

## Ground rules (and why)

- **Organic only.** Never suggest buying followers, follow/unfollow, engagement pods, comment bots or third-party "growth" apps that need the Instagram password. Instagram detects these, cuts reach or blocks the account, and fake followers never become customers. If the user asks for any of these, explain this briefly and offer the honest version.
- **No invented facts.** Phone, address, prices, opening hours and offers come from the user or from memory. Put `[price]` style placeholders where a detail is missing rather than guessing.
- **No guaranteed numbers.** Don't promise "1,000 followers in a month". Give realistic expectations: steady posting plus local engagement usually shows results over 6 to 12 weeks.
- **Respect customers.** Before/after shots must hide personal data on screens (photos, chats, passwords) and customer faces unless they agreed.

## 1. Weekly content plan

Build around four content pillars, mixed through the week so the feed is not just adverts:

1. **Fix it** (show the work): repair process, before/after, "what's inside this laptop". Builds trust.
2. **Teach it** (useful tips): "3 signs your hard disk is dying", "speed up a slow laptop". Gets saves and shares, which drive reach.
3. **Local + people**: the team, a happy customer (with permission), the shop front, Kannur landmarks, festival greetings (Onam, Vishu, Christmas, Eid). Makes the shop familiar.
4. **Offer**: a service highlight or a seasonal deal (exam season, back-to-school, monsoon damage). At most 1 in 5 posts, so followers don't tune out.

Default rhythm: 4 to 5 Reels/posts a week plus stories most days. Reels reach non-followers the most, so make them the majority. Suggested times (IST) for a Kerala audience: 12:30–2 pm and 7–9:30 pm; Sunday late morning also works. Tell the user to check their own Insights after a few weeks and shift times to when their followers are active.

Output format:

```
## Week plan: <dates or "Week 1">
Goal this week: <one line>

| Day | Time | Format | Pillar | Idea | Hook (first 2 seconds) |
|-----|------|--------|--------|------|------------------------|

Stories this week: <3–5 story ideas: polls, quizzes, behind the scenes>
To film this week: <shot list so they can record it in one go at the shop>
```

Keep each idea specific enough to film tomorrow ("film opening a dusty laptop fan, then the clean one, 12 s") rather than vague ("post repair content"). If the user has the kannur-reel-editor skill, mention that the filmed clips can be edited with it.

## 2. Captions + hashtags

Structure each caption:

1. **Hook line** in the language the audience reacts to most (Malayalam by default, short and conversational, Manglish is fine if the user writes that way).
2. **2–4 lines of value**: what was wrong, what was done, or the tip. Malayalam first, English below, or mixed, matching what the user prefers.
3. **Call to action**: one clear ask, such as "Save this for later", "Tag a friend whose laptop is slow", or "DM 'FIX' for a quote" / "📍 Visit us at [address]".
4. **Contact line**: e.g. `📞 +91 94475 40218 (WhatsApp) · 🌐 www.kannurcomputers.com`, plus `📍 Chakkarakal Bus Stand, Near Sana Fancy, Kannur`. Tag the location as Chakkarakal, Kannur so nearby people find the post.
5. **Hashtags**: 5 to 10, chosen from `references/hashtags.md`: a mix of local (Kannur/Kerala), service-specific and a couple of broader tech tags. Fewer, relevant tags beat 30 random ones.

Also suggest: on-screen text for the Reel cover, and the location tag ("Kannur, Kerala" or the shop's location page). Offer 2 caption variants when writing for a single post so the user can choose.

## 4. Engagement routine

A 15–20 minute daily routine the owner or staff can actually keep:

- Reply to every comment and DM the same day; the first hour after posting matters most.
- Spend 10 minutes engaging with **local** accounts: Kannur news/meme pages, colleges, coaching centres, cafés, other non-competing shops. Leave real comments, not "nice 👍".
- Post 2–3 stories: a poll ("Laptop or desktop?"), a quiz, work in progress, or reshare customer tags.
- Weekly: ask 1–2 happy customers to tag the shop or leave a Google review; reshare tags.
- Monthly: plan one collab or Live with a local page or college club (e.g. a "laptop care for students" Live), or a small giveaway (free cleaning/service check) where entry = follow + tag a friend from Kannur. Giveaways bring followers fast but some leave afterwards, so pair them with strong regular content.
- Review Insights every 2 weeks: which posts got most shares, saves and profile visits? Make more of those.

Output it as a simple checklist (daily / weekly / monthly) the user can screenshot.
