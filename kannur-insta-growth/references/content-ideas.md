# Content idea bank

Pick from these and make them specific to what the shop actually did this week.

## Fix it (show the work)
- Before/after: dusty laptop fan → cleaned; thermal paste replacement with temperature shown before and after
- "Guess the problem" Reel: show the symptom, ask viewers to comment, reveal the fix in the next Reel or a pinned comment
- Screen replacement in 15 s (sped up, satisfying cuts)
- Water-damaged laptop rescue (monsoon season)
- HDD → SSD upgrade with a boot-time comparison side by side
- Opening a laptop nobody has opened in 5 years
- Data recovered from a "dead" pen drive or hard disk (hide the actual files)
- Desktop/gaming PC build for a customer, start to finish
- Printer, CCTV or networking jobs if the shop does them

## Teach it (tips people save and share)
- 3 signs your hard disk is about to fail
- Why your laptop gets hot and how to stop it
- Laptop battery myths (keep it plugged in all day?)
- How to choose a laptop for students under ₹[budget]
- What to do in the first 5 minutes after spilling water on a laptop
- Keyboard shortcuts most people don't know
- How to back up WhatsApp/photos before giving your phone or laptop for repair
- Spotting a fake charger
- Free up storage on Windows in 2 minutes
- Common scam: "your computer has a virus" pop-ups

## Local + people
- Meet the technician: name, years of experience, favourite repair
- A day at the shop in 20 seconds
- Customer reaction when they get their data back (with permission)
- Shop location: "how to reach us: right at Chakkarakal bus stand, near Sana Fancy"
- Festival greetings: Onam, Vishu, Christmas, Eid, New Year, with a small tech twist
- Kannur-themed: "every Kannur student's laptop during exam week" meme
- Answering a real customer question from the DMs

## Offer (max 1 in 5 posts)
- Exam season: free check-up/cleaning for students with ID
- Back-to-school/college: laptop servicing combo
- Monsoon: water-damage inspection
- Festival offer
- New service launch
- Referral: bring a friend, both get [benefit]

## Seasonal calendar (Kerala)
- Mar–Apr: exam season, Vishu
- May–Jun: college admissions, laptop buying, monsoon starts
- Jun–Sep: monsoon water damage, power surge issues
- Aug–Sep: Onam
- Dec: Christmas, year-end sales

## Hooks that stop the scroll (first 2 seconds)
- "Don't throw away your old laptop yet…"
- "This is why your laptop is slow."
- "ഈ ലാപ്ടോപ്പ് ഇനി ഓൺ ആകില്ലെന്ന് പറഞ്ഞു…" (they said this laptop would never turn on again…)
- "Kannur, check your laptop fan right now."
- "₹[x] vs ₹[y] repair: what's the difference?"
